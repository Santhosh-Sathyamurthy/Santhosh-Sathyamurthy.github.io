Grievance redressal

If a club or student feels a Council decision (budget, venue, scheduling) was unfair, it can be raised formally rather than left unresolved:

  1. Raise it first with the relevant sub-council coordinator, in writing.
  2. If unresolved within 7 days, escalate to the General Secretary via this contact form (select "Something else" and mark it "Grievance").
  3. Unresolved grievances go to the Dean of Student Affairs, per the Constitution.
