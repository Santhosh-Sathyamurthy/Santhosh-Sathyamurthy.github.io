Notes for whoever picks the final one

  • Notebook is what's currently live across the site: a cool paper background with a notice-board yellow accent, built around the "index card" idea.
  • Indigo Campus reads a little more energetic and tech-forward, with a coral accent against deep indigo.
  • Terracotta Campus is the warmest option, closer to a lot of Indian college fest branding, with a burnt-orange accent on cream.
  • Monsoon Slate is the most restrained of the four, closest to a typical formal government site, with a slate-teal accent.
  • All four ship with working dark mode. Switching deck, font or mode here doesn't change anything outside this preview box: to make one of these the site's real theme, its values move from theme-decks.css into tokens.css.
