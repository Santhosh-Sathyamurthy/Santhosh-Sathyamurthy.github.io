Privacy Policy

Written to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and GIGW 3.0's privacy transparency requirements. Review with the institute's data protection officer before launch.

What we collect

This site does not require login or store personal data by default. The only personal data collected is what you choose to submit through the contact form: name, roll number (optional), institute email, phone (optional) and your message.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to club registrations, event submissions, volunteering requests and general queries. It is not used for any other purpose, sold, or shared outside the Council of Student Activities and the relevant sub-council coordinator.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. It uses your browser's localStorage only to remember your accessibility preferences (text size, dark/light mode) and a local visit counter. None of this leaves your device or identifies you. See Accessibility Statement for the accessibility controls, and assets/js/visitor-counter.js for exactly how the counter works.

Third-party links

Where this site links to an external service (for example, Google Maps or social media), that service's own privacy policy applies once you leave this site. We flag external links clearly rather than embedding third-party trackers directly into pages. See the Hyperlinking Policy.

Your rights

You may ask the Council to access, correct or delete any information you submitted through the contact form, by emailing studentactivities@iisertirupati.ac.in.

Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to action the request (typically one semester), after which they are deleted from any working document or inbox folder used to track them.
