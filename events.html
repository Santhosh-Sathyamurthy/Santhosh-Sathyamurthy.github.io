Planning an event?

How your event gets on this calendar

  1. Your club fills the event submission form at least two weeks before the date.
  2. The Council checks for venue and date clashes across all clubs. First submitted, first held, with ties resolved by a joint slot rather than by which club is "bigger".
  3. Once confirmed, it's added to data/events.json and appears here automatically, in its correct chronological place.
