Governance

The Council of Student Activities

The Council is the elected student body that coordinates every club at IISER Tirupati, allocating budgets, resolving venue and date clashes, and representing student activities to the institute administration.

How it's structured

Five sub-councils sit under the main Council, one per category of club activity. Each sub-council has one coordinator, elected by the clubs within it, who sits on the main Council.

Elected

General Secretary, Student Activities

Chairs the Council, casts the deciding vote on shared-resource disputes, and is the point of contact with the Dean of Student Affairs.

Elected annually by the student body

Elected

Five Sub-council Coordinators

One per category (Cultural, Literary & Media, Social & Wellness, Sports, Technical), elected by the clubs in that category.

Elected each semester

Names of the current office-bearers will be published here once the ongoing election cycle concludes, rather than being placeholder-filled with invented names.

Site policy

No club is favoured on this site

This is a stated, checkable policy for anyone maintaining this site after us, not a one-off promise:

  • Ordering. Councils are listed alphabetically everywhere (Cultural, Literary & Media, Social & Wellness, Sports, Technical). Clubs are listed alphabetically within their council. Events are listed strictly by date. There is no "featured" flag anywhere in the underlying data.
  • Homepage spotlight. The three-club preview on the homepage shows one club per council and rotates which club is shown, using the day of the year as a seed, so it changes daily but stays fully deterministic and auditable, never a manual pick.
  • Imagery. No club gets a real photo while others get a placeholder. Every club currently gets the same generated pattern art until real photos are supplied for all of them at once.
  • Event scheduling. Venue and date clashes are resolved first-submitted-first-held, or by a joint slot, never by club size or funding.
  • Changing this policy. Any change to the ordering or selection logic in clubs-render.js or events-render.js should be raised with the full Council, not made unilaterally by whoever is maintaining the site that semester.
Governing document

Constitution, in brief

The full constitution is held by the institute administration; this is a plain-language summary for students.

Any group of at least ten registered students, with a named convenor and a faculty advisor, can apply. See the start-a-club form.

Each sub-council receives an equal base allocation per semester, split further by clubs' submitted activity plans rather than by club size or past spending.

Venue, date or budget disputes go to the relevant sub-council coordinator first, and to the General Secretary if unresolved. Decisions are logged and available to any club on request.
