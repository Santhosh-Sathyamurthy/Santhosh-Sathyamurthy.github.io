Hyperlinking Policy

This is a working draft covering how linking to and from this site should be handled. The institute administration may adjust the specifics before the site is treated as final.

Links to this website

Other websites are welcome to link to any page here without prior permission, provided the link does not suggest false endorsement by IISER Tirupati and the linking page does not display objectionable, illegal or misleading content alongside the link.

Links from this website

Where this site links to an external site (the main institute site, social media, Google Maps, club-run pages, etc.), that link is clearly marked and opens with rel="noopener noreferrer". Presence of a link does not imply this site's endorsement of the external content, and we are not responsible for the accuracy, availability or content of external sites once you leave.

Why we keep external links to a minimum

In line with UX4G guidance for government-linked sites, we avoid embedding third-party widgets (maps, video players, social feeds) directly into pages, since these can load trackers without the visitor's awareness. Where an external resource is genuinely useful (for example, a campus map), we link out to it explicitly rather than embedding it. See the campus map link on the Contact page.

Framing

Pages from this site should not be presented within a frame on another website without permission from the Council of Student Activities.
